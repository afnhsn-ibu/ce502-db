## Midterm exam

The midterm exam takes place after week 6 and covers weeks 1 to 6: transaction logs, backup and recovery, concurrency control, distributed architectures, horizontal fragmentation and vertical fragmentation. It is a closed-book written exam of 90 minutes. Students may bring one double-sided A4 page of handwritten notes and a non-programmable calculator.

## Lab environment

All labs use Docker. Students install Docker Desktop in week 1 and use PostgreSQL 16 for weeks 1 to 7, DuckDB in week 8, MongoDB and Cassandra in week 9, Chroma in week 10 and PostgreSQL with the pgvector extension in weeks 11 and 12. Python 3.10 or newer is required from week 6 onwards. Lab machines in room [room] have everything pre-installed.

## Office hours and contact

The instructor holds office hours every Wednesday from 14:00 to 16:00 in room [office]. Questions about labs should first be posted in the course forum, where teaching assistants answer within two working days. Personal matters such as extensions are handled by email.
