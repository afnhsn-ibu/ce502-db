## Course overview

CE 502 Advanced Topics in Database Systems runs for twelve weeks. The course moves from a single database (transaction logging, backup and recovery, concurrency control) to distributed databases (architecture, horizontal and vertical fragmentation), then to analytics and scale (data warehouses, big data, NoSQL) and finally to AI-era databases (embeddings, vector search and retrieval-augmented generation).

## Final project

Teams of two or three students build a retrieval-augmented generation application or a recommender system on a vector database of their choice. The project proposal is due in week 11 and the demo, repository and report are due in week 12. Each team presents for 10 minutes followed by 5 minutes of questions. The project must include a data pipeline, a justified choice of vector database and index, an evaluation of retrieval quality and a link to at least one earlier topic of the course.

## Careers assignment

In week 1 each student chooses one database career, such as database administrator, data architect, data engineer or AI engineer, and studies three real job postings. The 600 to 800 word report is due in week 3 and is peer-reviewed by three classmates using a 20-point rubric.
