## Grading

The final grade is made of weekly labs (30 percent), quizzes (10 percent), the careers assignment (5 percent), the midterm exam (25 percent) and the final project (30 percent). A student must score at least 40 percent on the midterm and the final project combined to pass the course.

## Late submission policy

Labs submitted up to 48 hours late lose 20 percent of their marks. Labs submitted more than 48 hours late receive zero unless an extension was approved in advance. Each student has one free extension of 72 hours for any single lab, which must be requested by email before the deadline. Quizzes cannot be taken late.

## Academic integrity

Students may discuss labs with classmates but must write their own code and reports. Using AI assistants is allowed for the final project if every use is listed in an appendix of the report. Copying another team's code or submitting generated text without acknowledgement is plagiarism and is reported to the faculty.
