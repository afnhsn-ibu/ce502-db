## Guidance for good reviews

- **Be specific.** Point to a section, a table row or an advert number. "Section 4.3 shows Kubernetes in A2 only, so it is a weak signal for the learning plan" is useful; "nice report" is not.
- **Score the report, not the person.** Use the rubric descriptors, not your opinion of the role.
- **Check, don't assume.** At least one quoted requirement per report must be verified against the advert.
- **Be respectful.** Write feedback you would be happy to receive.
- **Do not change your scores after discussing with the author.** If you think you made a mistake, tell the instructor.

The instructor reviews all peer scores and sets the final mark. Reviews that are missing, late or not specific cost the reviewer 2 points from their own report mark.
