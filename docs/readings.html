Papers to read

Each week has two or three required papers and two or three optional ones. The deck says which sections to read. For every required paper, bring a half-page note to the next class: the problem, the key idea, and how it connects to the week. Use the paper note template. One quiz question each week may refer to the required reading.

In practice Week 1 teaches Keshav’s three-pass method. Use the first pass on every paper here, and all three on the ones you build your final project on.
