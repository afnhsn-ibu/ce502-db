Syllabus

CE 502 is a graduate (master's) course at International Balkan University, Skopje. It assumes an undergraduate databases course: you can design a relational schema and write SQL joins and aggregates. Everything beyond that is built in the lessons.

The course follows one story: one database that must survive crashes and concurrent users (Weeks 1–3), many databases that must behave like one (Weeks 4–6), analytics and scale (Weeks 7–9), and AI-era databases that search by meaning (Weeks 10–12).

One session of about three hours each week: three 45-minute lessons and a practice.

How a week runs

  • Three lessons of 45 minutes, each with a scenario worked in pairs and one Check Your Understanding question.
  • One practice in the course database, started in class and finished as homework, with a short report.
  • A quiz of 15–16 multiple-choice questions, plus one short design question from Week 2 on.
  • Two or three required papers, with a half-page note due at the next class.

Using AI tools

You may use an AI assistant to explain concepts, debug your own code and SQL, and improve your writing. You must be able to explain every line you submit. Work you cannot explain is treated as work you did not do.

State in your submission which tool you used and what for. That statement costs you no marks. Omitting it does. Lab reports ask for numbers you measured in your own container; numbers that do not match your run are marked as missing.
